# 📊 RavenStack Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer retention and churn for RavenStack, a SaaS subscription business.

The objective is to identify why customers leave, determine which customer segments have the highest churn risk, understand customer lifetime and retention patterns, and provide actionable recommendations to improve retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

- What is the overall customer churn rate?
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
- Which acquisition channels have better retention?
- What are the main reasons customers leave?
- How long do customers typically remain active?
- What actions could help reduce customer churn?

---

## 📂 Dataset

The analysis uses five datasets:

- `ravenstack_accounts.csv`
- `ravenstack_subscriptions.csv`
- `ravenstack_feature_usage.csv`
- `ravenstack_churn_events.csv`
- `ravenstack_support_tickets.csv`

The datasets contain customer profiles, subscriptions, product usage,
churn events, and customer support information.

## 📈 Key Results

### Overall Churn Rate

- Total Customers: 500
- Churned Customers: 110
- Retained Customers: 390
- Churn Rate: 22.0%
- Median Customer Lifetime: ~298 days

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. DevTools Customers Have Higher Churn

DevTools customers have a churn rate of approximately 31%, making
them one of the highest-risk customer segments.

### 2. Acquisition Channel Matters

Event-acquired customers have approximately 30.2% churn, compared
with approximately 14.6% for partner-acquired customers.

This suggests customer acquisition quality differs significantly by channel.

### 3. Multiple Factors Drive Churn

Important recorded churn reasons include:

- Budget
- Pricing
- Features
- Support
- Competitors

This indicates that churn is influenced by product, customer experience,
and affordability rather than one single factor.

### 4. Customer Lifetime

The median customer lifetime is approximately 298 days.

Customer lifetime analysis can help identify when customers are most
likely to leave and where retention interventions may be most effective.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Create a targeted retention strategy for DevTools customers.
2. Review the quality of customers acquired through events.
3. Improve trial-to-paid onboarding and early product activation.
4. Investigate feature gaps mentioned by churned customers.
5. Improve customer support for accounts with repeated issues.
6. Offer downgrade or retention options to price-sensitive customers.

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis found an overall customer churn rate of 22%.

Churn is concentrated in specific customer segments and acquisition
channels, particularly DevTools customers and event-acquired customers.

The results suggest that RavenStack can improve retention through better
customer onboarding, product adoption, support experience, acquisition
quality, and targeted retention strategies.
